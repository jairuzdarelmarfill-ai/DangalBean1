Shop Overview

Our coffee shop is a cozy and welcoming place where customers can enjoy freshly brewed coffee, delicious pastries, and relaxing moments. We offer a variety of coffee drinks, including espresso, latte, cappuccino, and iced coffee, along with tasty snacks.

Our goal is to provide quality products, friendly service, and a comfortable for everyone. Whether you want to study, meet friends, work, or simply enjoy a cup of coffee, our shop is the perfect place to relax and enjoy.

Welcome to Dangal Beans

We built Dangal Beans for one simple reason. PNC students needed an affordable place where they could grab a good cup of coffee, study, relax, and get their schoolwork done. Whether you're preparing for an exam, finishing a project, meeting friends, or simply taking a breather between classes, Dangal Beans is a welcoming place for PNCians and everyone in the community.
