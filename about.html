About Dangal Beans

Get to know the story, the purpose, and the people behind the coffee shop created with PNCians and the community in mind.

Why We Created Dangal Beans

Dangal Beans started with a simple observation. PNC students needed an affordable place to study, relax, and recharge with a good cup of coffee. Coffee shops near campus were often too expensive or too far, so we brought the coffee shop experience closer to the community in Windfield, Cabuyao. Created by PNCians, Dangal Beans welcomes everyone.


Our purpose is simple. We aim to support student life while serving the wider community. Whether you're reviewing for an exam, finishing a group project, meeting friends, grabbing a quick coffee, or simply taking a breather between classes, Dangal Beans is designed to be a welcoming part of everyone's everyday routine.

A Student-Friendly Environment

Every part of Dangal Beans is designed with students in mind — from our pricing to our layout. We keep the space quiet enough to focus, with free Wi-Fi and charging stations at nearly every table, so you can stay productive for as long as you need.
